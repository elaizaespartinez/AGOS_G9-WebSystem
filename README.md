<h1 align="center">🌊 A.G.O.S. — Abiso at Gabay sa Oras ng Sakuna</h1>
<i>"Sa bawat AGOS, may gabay."</i>
<br/><br/>
<b>AGOS</b> is a nationwide disaster web system that monitors floodwater levels and delivers timely, life-saving information to communities across the Philippines. Registered users receive flood alerts by SMS or email, find safer commuting routes, locate evacuation centers, and reach help fast, while administrators manage flood data and rescue operations in real time.
</div>
 
## 💭 Overview
 
Tuwing may bagyo o malakas na ulan, ang pinakamahalaga ay **maagang abiso** at **malinaw na gabay**.
 
**AGOS** connects communities and responders in one place. Admins input flood radius and affected areas, assign rescue teams and rescue points, and send alerts. Residents get notified, see which barangays and routes are affected, check where the nearest evacuation center is, and plan a safer way to get around.
 
**Who is it for?**
 
- 🏘️ **Residents**: receive alerts, find evacuation centers, get alternative routes
- 🛟 **Rescue teams and volunteers**: know where help is needed
- 🧑‍💼 **Administrators**: manage flood data, barangays, alerts, and rescue assignments
- 🤝 **Donors and volunteers**: help communities in need
</br>
**Features:**
 
- 🚨 **Alert System**: registered users get SMS or email alerts for their barangay
- 🗺️ **Transport and Commuting Alternatives**: alternative routes when roads are flooded
- 📍 **Flood Radius Management**: admin inputs the flood radius and affected zones
- 🛟 **Rescue Coordination**: admin sets rescue team and place of rescue
- 🏫 **Evacuation Center Directory**: find the nearest evacuation centers per barangay
- 🤖 **Chatbot**: quick answers and guidance during emergencies
- 📞 **Emergency Hotline**: important numbers one tap away
- 💛 **Help and Donation**: donate money or sign up as a volunteer
- 🔐 **User Accounts**: registration, login, and personal profiles
- 🇵🇭 **Nationwide Coverage**: not limited to a single barangay or city
## 👥 User Roles
 
| Role | What they can do |
|------|------------------|
| **Resident (User)** | Register, receive alerts, view evacuation centers and routes, use chatbot, donate or volunteer |
| **Admin** | Manage barangays, flood-prone zones and hazards, evacuation centers, transport routes, send alerts, assign rescue teams |
